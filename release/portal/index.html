🚇 Global Subway Network

Dual-view rendering with complete transit topology.

  • Schematic Diagram View: Interactive, pinch-to-zoom vectorized lines with transfer hubs.
  • Geographic Map View: Station nodes plotted on true GIS coordinates.
  • Station Details: Multilingual names, code, operating lines, first/last trains, elevator & wheelchair accessibility, exit landmarks.

🚌 Real-time Bus & Multimodal

Seamless journey planning from hotel door to landmark.

  • Multimodal Engine: Walk ➔ Metro ➔ Bus ➔ Walk unified instructions.
  • Real-time Arrival States: Distinct badges for LIVE (GPS), SCHEDULED, and ESTIMATED.
  • Preference Engine: Fastest, Minimum Transfers, Least Walking, or Accessible route filters.

🏛️ Destination & Verified Reviews

Direct linkage between attractions, transit, and weather.

  • Article 12 Compliance: Landmark details display exact nearest metro exit and bus stop.
  • Destination Weather: Real-time temperature & forecast for every attraction.
  • Verified Visit Reviews: Authentic tips, spam prevention, and rating breakdown.
